Ten ways metered energy data pays back

Cost and penalties

Lower energy bills

Exposes idle loads, after-hours running and inefficient assets. Typically 10–20% consumption reduction in the first year.

Cost and penalties

Maximum demand control

Live MD tracking with alerts before the contracted limit is breached. Avoids demand penalties and allows a lower contract demand at renewal.

Cost and penalties

Power factor management

Continuous PF monitoring per feeder, with capacitor bank failure flagged early. Avoids PF penalty and captures incentive.

Cost and penalties

Bill verification

Utility invoice reconciled against your own metered data, catching wrong multipliers, slab errors and incorrect demand charges.

Visibility and accountability

Load-level visibility

Actual kWh and kVA per feeder, machine and area in real time. Decisions on measured data, not one monthly bill.

Visibility and accountability

Specific energy consumption

kWh per tonne or per unit produced, tracked by line and shift. Isolates the inefficient shift, line or product mix.

Visibility and accountability

Cost allocation and accountability

Consumption mapped to department, line or cost centre, making energy a controllable line item with a named owner.

Reliability

Power quality monitoring

Harmonics, voltage sags, unbalance and transients logged with timestamps. Explains motor overheating, drive tripping and unexplained line stoppages.

Reliability

Early fault detection

Abnormal consumption or current signatures flag failing equipment, such as choked filters, bearing wear and insulation issues, before breakdown.

Savings and compliance

Savings verification and compliance

Baseline vs post-implementation comparison proves the ROI of every efficiency project, and the same data serves ISO 50001, BEE PAT and Scope 2 reporting.

Architecture

From meter to decision, in the cloud or on premise

Standard energy meters on RS485 Modbus feed industrial gateways in each substation. Choose where the platform runs; the meters and panels stay the same.

Cloud deployment

Each substation sends its data over 4G, Wi-Fi or the plant network to I/O Sense Cloud.

I/O Sense platform

Eight modules, configured without code

Everything from user access to custom KPIs is set up by your own team through the interface.

Admin PanelDevicesCompute ReportsTriggersI/O LensI/O MatrixExpression ResolverSub-users
01

Admin Panel

Asset mapping and user creation

Manage and create users, assign roles, configure devices and control feature access. The full-stack manager for devices, assets and AI applications.

  • Onboard, configure and control device and asset access across users; categorise sensors, set parameters and calibrate their data
  • Create user accounts, assign quotas and allocate devices, reports and permissions
  • Allocate features straight to a user account for fast deployment
  • Host I/O Sense for multiple domains, each with its assets and features kept isolated
02

Devices

Individual device analysis

Explore and visualise real-time data and historical trends of every parameter collected from each asset.

  • Phase-wise current and voltage, live and as trends
  • Power factor, active, reactive and apparent power, frequency
  • Daily and hourly active energy trends
  • Works for any energy meter, flow meter or sensor
03

Compute Reports

Excel reports and scheduler

Create customised Excel reports, schedule them on mail and store them for later access.

  • Drag-and-drop compute reports, generated and downloaded on demand
  • Scheduled delivery to designated email IDs
  • Change formulas across sheets without any coding
  • Hourly consumption and daily in-depth consumption analysis, with a full report log
04

Triggers

Events and alerts

Configure SMS, email and call alerts on conditions or thresholds, sent to multiple stakeholders from a simple interface.

  • Alerts on full-load current, low efficiency, process parameter deviation or high consumption
  • Event analytics: which alerts fired, where and for how long
  • Every alert message and every configuration change logged
05

I/O Lens

No-code dashboard builder

Drag-and-drop dashboard builder for operator, supervisor and management dashboards, on real-time or historical data.

  • Pre-built widgets: data cards, single and multi-line charts, bar, pie and combined charts, tables and more
  • Upload plant images and animations and overlay live widgets on them
  • Section-wise dashboards: total energy, SEC, production, idle hours and energy wasted while idle, with benchmarking and prediction
  • Dynamic SLDs: single-line diagrams overlaid with live data to see power distribution in real time
06

I/O Matrix

360-degree drill-down analysis

Drill-down analysis of energy consumption, power usage, specific energy consumption (SEC) and other operational parameters.

  • Trends and benchmarks for plant energy, water, steam, specific power and efficiency
  • Section-wise and equipment-wise consumption trends
  • Group assets, define hierarchy and formulas
  • Compare energy, power and demand, and SEC at monthly, daily, hourly, 30-minute and 15-minute intervals
  • Benchmarking and prediction based on production and SEC
07

Expression Resolver

Custom formulas and KPIs

Create and manage custom formulas that calculate and store KPIs and other metrics from raw data, then use them in dashboards and reports.

  • Any formula, applied once and reused across I/O Lens, dashboards, devices, I/O Matrix and reports
  • Users manage their own computations and formulas
  • Formula creator with built-in operations and a library of saved expressions
08

Sub-users

Role-based access control

Role-based access that controls, feature by feature, what each kind of user can see and do.

  • Give other users limited or read-only access to a main account
  • Create roles with view, create, update and delete rights per feature
  • One login can be a sub-user of several main accounts and switch between them
