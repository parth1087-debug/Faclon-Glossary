I/O Sense platform key features

Diagnoses and actions, not just data

Every monitored asset carries a risk level, the fault found and the recommended solution, with the full vibration spectrum one click away.

I/O Sense platform

Automated diagnostics & maintenance recommendations

Detected anomalies are automatically analyzed, generating issue descriptions, severity classification, and recommended corrective actions (e.g., alignment check, bearing inspection, lubrication, balancing). This helps maintenance teams quickly identify root causes and take targeted action.

I/O Sense platform

Advanced signal processing & fault detection

The system performs continuous FFT spectrum analysis, harmonic detection, envelope analysis, and trend monitoring to identify vibration signatures associated with imbalance, misalignment, looseness, bearing defects, gear wear, cavitation, resonance, and other mechanical faults.
